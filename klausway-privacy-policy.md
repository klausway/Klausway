## 1. Scope of this Privacy Policy {#scope}

This Privacy Policy (the "**Policy**") describes how Klaus Way and our affiliated companies (hereinafter "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**") collect, use, and disclose personal information when you visit our website at klausway.com (the "**Site**"), request a free consultation, apply for a job with us, or otherwise contact us about our IT consulting, custom application development, system integration, automation, data analytics, or cloud services (collectively, the "**Consulting Services**").

For purposes of this Policy, "**you**" means a visitor to our Site or a prospective client, vendor, or job applicant.

**Important note on client and project data.** If you engage Klaus Way for Consulting Services, we may build, host, or support custom software applications on your behalf (for example, a CRM, e-signature tool, reporting system, or similar "Client Application"). Where we process personal information within a Client Application, or otherwise process data on your behalf as part of a project, we generally do so as a service provider/processor acting on your instructions, under the confidentiality and data-processing terms of the applicable Services Agreement and/or Data Processing Addendum ("**DPA**") — not under this Policy. This Policy instead covers (a) personal information we collect from visitors to our Site and prospective clients, and (b) personal information of our clients' authorized contacts needed to manage the business relationship.

**By accessing or using our Site, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy, and you consent to our collection and use of your personal information as described below.** If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

Where required by law, we will obtain additional consent through opt-in mechanisms such as cookie banners or checkboxes. For your rights regarding cookies, see the [Cookie Notice](#cookie-notice) below, which forms part of this Policy.

Our Site may link to other websites, including client and partner sites shown in our portfolio. The privacy practices of those sites are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We Collect {#info-we-collect}

**2.3. See our [Cookie Notice](#cookie-notice) for details.

## Cookie Notice {#cookie-notice}

**Last updated: July 30, 2026**

This Cookie Notice, part of our Privacy Policy, explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on our Site.

**What are cookies?** Cookies are small data files placed on your device by your browser when you visit a website, used to remember information about your visit so your next visit is more convenient.

**What kinds of cookies do we use?**

- **Strictly necessary cookies** — required for the Site to function properly (for example, maintaining basic session state). These cannot be switched off and do not require consent.
- **Analytics/performance cookies** — help us understand how visitors use our Site (such as which pages are viewed and time on page) so we can improve it. We may use tools such as Google Analytics.
- **Functionality/preference cookies** — remember choices you've made to provide a more personalized browsing experience.
- **Advertising/targeting cookies** — used, where enabled, to measure and improve the effectiveness of our marketing and to show relevant ads about our services on other sites.

**Using cookies with your consent.** Where required by law, we will ask for your consent to non-essential cookies via a cookie banner on your first visit, and you may change your preferences at any time through that banner or your browser settings.

**Managing your cookie settings.** Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Because strictly necessary cookies are required for the Site to work, you cannot refuse them without affecting functionality. You can opt out of interest-based advertising generally through industry tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance (https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or the Network Advertising Initiative (https://thenai.org/), and out of Google Analytics specifically via Google's opt-out tools.

**Changes to this Cookie Notice.** We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use. The date above reflects the most recent update.

If you have questions about this Cookie Notice, contact us using the information in [How to Contact Us](#contact).
